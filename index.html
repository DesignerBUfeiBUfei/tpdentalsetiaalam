DR KELVIN QUAH (I.D.G, USU)

Dr Kelvin is a dental surgeon with a decade of experience. He graduated from Universitas Sumatera Utara in 2016. After returning to Malaysia, he was posted to Langkawi, where he served at Klinik Pergigian Air Hangat and Hospital Sultanah Maliha, focusing on outpatient and trauma cases. He also served as a junior Dental Officer in the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Department and Paediatric Dental Department at Hospital Sultanah Bahiyah.

After leaving the Ministry of Health in 2019, Dr Kelvin became a full-time private practitioner, focusing mainly on general dentistry, dentures and minor oral surgery.
